Redline Check Compare

Put the earlier version of a file on the left and the later version on the right. Every change is listed with its page, paragraph or cell, and you can download a marked-up copy.

Files are read inside your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.
vs
Works with .pdf (text-based, not scanned), .docx, .xlsx / .xls / .csv and .txt. Two different formats can be compared too, for example a PDF against its Word draft. Old .doc files must first be saved as .docx.
